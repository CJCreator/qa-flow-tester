How we make kaju katli

Ingredients

  • 500 g cashews, soaked for one hour
  • 250 g sugar and 120 ml water
  • Two tablespoons of ghee
  • Silver leaf to finish

Method

  1. Drain the cashews and grind them to a fine, dry powder.
  2. Boil the sugar and water to a one-string syrup.
  3. Stir in the cashew powder on a low flame until it leaves the sides of the pan.
  4. Knead with ghee, roll thin, cover with silver leaf and cut into diamonds.
