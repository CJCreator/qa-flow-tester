About Kaveri Sweets

Meenakshi Raman opened Kaveri Sweets as a two-table shop on Lake View Road in 1985. Her grandchildren run it today, and the Mysore pak is still made to her recipe, with ghee from a dairy in Kancheepuram.

How we cook

We cook in small batches each morning and sell out most days. Nothing is kept overnight, so ask us before noon for large orders.

Prices

Prices change with the season. See our old price list or ask in the shop.
