Subject: Try Release check-up on your website (a few minutes)

Hi,

I would like you to try Release check-up. It looks at your website, writes a Plan of what to test, runs the Check-up and tells you in plain words whether the website is ready to release, with the problems in priority order. No QA team needed.

Start here: https://qa-flow-tester.vercel.app/get-started

What you need:

- A Google account to sign in.
- Your own AI key. A free Gemini key takes a minute to make at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey. The page above shows where to paste it.
- The public address of a website you own or have permission to check.

Good to know:

- The first visit after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to wake up. Keep the page open.
- By default it only looks at your website. It does not fill in or send forms. Form, sign-in and security checks unlock only after you prove you control the website (a Verified Domain), and the page explains how.
- Your AI key is kept in memory for your visit only, so paste it again next time.
- Your reports are kept for 30 days, then deleted.
- There is a daily limit per person and one Check-up runs at a time, so you may wait in a short line.

This is a pilot, so things may break. Tell me what confused you or what went wrong: a reply to this message is enough.

Thank you,
[Your name]
